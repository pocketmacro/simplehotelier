The short version

  • We collect what's needed to run your property: your account, your property's details, your reservations and the guests who book with you.
  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Card numbers go straight to Stripe. We only keep the card brand and last four digits.
  • Guest details belong to the property they booked with. We look after them on the property's behalf.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it at support@simplehotelier.com.

1. Who we are

Pocket Macro LLC ("we", "us"), which owns and operates SimpleHotelier, runs simplehotelier.com, the SimpleHotelier dashboard used by cabins, inns and small hotels, and the booking pages those properties publish for their guests.

This policy covers two kinds of information:

  • Information about our customers: the property owners and team members who use SimpleHotelier, and visitors to our website. We decide how this is used.
  • Information about guests: people who book a stay through a property's booking page, or whose booking a property records in SimpleHotelier. The property decides how guest information is used, and we process it for them under our Terms of Service. See If you booked a stay.

2. What we collect

From property owners and their teams

  • Account details: email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we can't see them.
  • Team: the email addresses you invite and the role each person has.
  • Property details: name, address, contact details, photos, logo, rooms, rates, policies, taxes, add-ons and booking page settings.
  • Billing: your subscription status and Stripe customer reference. Your card is held by Stripe, not by us.
  • Support: the support cases you open and the questions you ask Spark, our support assistant.

About guests

  • Name, email address and phone number.
  • Postal address, if the property asks for it.
  • Stay details: dates, room, number of adults, children and infants, arrival time, add-ons, notes and the booking's status.
  • Payments: amounts, refunds and, for card payments, the card brand, last four digits and Stripe references. Card numbers are entered into Stripe's secure form and never reach our servers.
  • Whether the guest opted in to the property's marketing emails, when the property offers that choice.

Collected automatically

  • Error reports: when something breaks in the dashboard or a booking page, the page sends us the error message, the page address, the browser type and the property involved, so we can fix it.
  • Abuse protection: to limit bursts of bookings and repeated promo-code guesses, we keep a one-way hash of the connecting IP address (never the address itself) for a day. Cloudflare Turnstile checks booking requests for bots.
  • Website analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to our website without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

3. How we use it

  • To run the service: taking bookings, keeping calendars in sync, recording payments and showing your team what needs doing.
  • To send the emails the service depends on: booking confirmations, payment receipts, check-in and check-out reminders, weekly reports to owners, team invites, and sign-in emails such as password resets.
  • To bill your subscription and process guest payments through Stripe.
  • To answer support requests and fix problems.
  • To keep the service secure and stop fraud and abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use guest information for our own marketing.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small set of service providers to run SimpleHotelier. Each only receives what it needs for its job.

CloudflareHosts our website and booking pages, Turnstile bot checks, and cookie-free website analytics.
StripeSubscription billing, and guest card payments through each property's own connected Stripe account.

We also share information:

  • With the property a guest books with, including everything the guest enters on the booking page.
  • With calendar services the property connects, such as Airbnb or Vrbo. We read the property's calendar from them and publish a calendar link the property can give them. That link shows booked dates only, not guest names.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, guests or us.
  • If our business changes hands, with the new owner, who must keep honoring this policy.

5. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The dashboard keeps your sign-in session and a few preferences (such as which property you last opened) in your browser's storage so you stay signed in.

Stripe and Cloudflare Turnstile may set cookies that they need for fraud prevention and bot checks. A booking page only sets Google Analytics cookies if the property has added its own Google Analytics ID.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, property and reservation data: while your account is open. After you cancel, we keep it so you can come back or export it, and delete it when you ask us to.
  • Guest records: for as long as the property keeps them in its account.
  • Payment records: as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Error reports: 30 days.
  • Hashed IP addresses used for abuse protection: 1 day.

7. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Each property's data is kept apart by database access rules, and team members only see what their role allows: housekeeping staff, for example, don't see payments. Card details are handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • limit or object to how we use it.

Email support@simplehotelier.com from the address on your account, or open a case from Support in the dashboard. We'll confirm it's you and reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. If you're in Europe or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Owners can export their reservations at any time from Reservations → Export CSV.

9. If you booked a stay

The property you booked with is responsible for your information, and its own policies apply to how it uses it. For questions about your booking or requests about your data, contact the property first: its contact details are on your confirmation email. If you can't reach them, email us at support@simplehotelier.com and we'll help.

10. Children

SimpleHotelier is a tool for businesses and isn't meant for children, and bookings should be made by adults. A booking may record how many children are coming, but not their names. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 16 in Europe).

11. Changes and contact

If we make a significant change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell account owners by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.

Questions or requests: support@simplehotelier.com.
