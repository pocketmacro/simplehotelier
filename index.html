Common questions

Do I need my own website?

No. Your booking page lives on its own address and you can share the link anywhere. If you do have a website, the embed snippet drops the same booking flow into it.

How do I get paid by guests?

You connect your own Stripe account during setup. Guest payments settle to you, and SimpleHotelier never holds your money.

Can my cleaners use it on their phones?

Yes. The housekeeping board is built for phones. Cleaners see only the rooms they need and flag them clean as they finish.

What if I need help?

Open Support inside the app. The AI assistant answers how-to questions instantly, and anything it cannot solve becomes a support case our team can see and reply to.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from billing settings. Your data stays readable until the end of the paid month.
