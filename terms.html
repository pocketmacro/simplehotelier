5. Guest bookings and payments

SimpleHotelier is software. When guests book with you, the booking and the stay are between you and the guest. That means you're responsible for:

  • your rates, availability, house rules, rental terms and cancellation policy, and for honoring them;
  • the accuracy of everything on your booking page;
  • licences, permits and rules that apply to renting your property;
  • collecting and paying occupancy, sales and other taxes. The tax figures SimpleHotelier shows are estimates to help you, not tax advice.

Guest card payments go through Stripe into your own connected Stripe account. To take them, you agree to Stripe's Connected Account Agreement. Stripe's processing fee on each guest payment is yours: it comes out of the payment before it reaches you, and SimpleHotelier keeps none of it. By default, a card processing fee covering it (at most 3% of the payment) is added to what the guest pays, shown to them before they pay and refunded in proportion with any refund; you can turn this off in your booking page settings. Card surcharges aren't allowed everywhere, so it's your responsibility to check they're allowed where you operate. Cards that cost Stripe more than the standard rate, such as cards from other countries, are settled from your payouts afterwards. You're responsible for refunds, chargebacks and disputes on your guests' payments, including any dispute fees. If a refund or chargeback on one of your guest payments is taken from us, we may recover that amount from your connected account or your future payouts.
